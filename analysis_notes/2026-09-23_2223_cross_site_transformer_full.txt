HEADLINE FINDING (confirmed with the real full-trained dissertation model,
not a quick-mode proxy):
1. Cross-site zero-shot transfer genuinely works: R2 0.78-0.98 with ZERO
   retraining, across two totally different plants (different continent,
   climate, capacity, instrumentation, sampling rate).
2. Leakage (current features) helps in-site AND cross-site, both
   directions - it never hurts.
3. THE GAP IS MUCH BIGGER CROSS-SITE THAN IN-SITE. In-site, leaky only
   adds ~0.017-0.018 R2 (both plants are already near ceiling on weather
   alone). Cross-site, leaky adds 0.10-0.18 R2 - an order of magnitude
   bigger effect.
   Reasonable physical read (not yet proven, just consistent with P=V*I
   being a site-invariant physical law while weather->power depends on
   site-specific tilt/azimuth/aerosols/soiling/efficiency): current
   features anchor the model in physics that transfers across sites,
   while a pure weather-to-power mapping is more site-specific and
   suffers more under domain shift.
